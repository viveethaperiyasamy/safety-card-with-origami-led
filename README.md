                   **SAFETY CARD**

**Aim**  
     To  create a safety card to prevent accidents and emergencies using colour paper and LED.

**Components Required**:

* Colour paper  
* Coin Cell Battery  
* Aluminium foil  u
* LED bulb  
    

**Working**  
      The safety card works by using a small LED light and a switch to indicate a safety alert. When the switch is pressed, the circuit is completed and the LED glows, giving a clear visual indication. The card can be used to create awareness about safety precautions and emergency situations.  
   
**Procedure**  
 1\. First, prepare a chart paper or thick paper as the base for the safety card.  
 2\. Draw and cut the required safety symbols and shapes using coloured paper.  
 3\. Arrange and paste the cut-out shapes neatly on the card.  
 4\. Draw safety-related symbols and instructions using a marker.  
 5\. Attach a small LED/light source and connect it to a battery through a switch.  
 6\. Fix the switch at the bottom of the card for easy operation.  
 7\. Press the switch to check whether the light glows properly.  
 8\. Finally, test the safety card and ensure that all the components are securely fixed.

**Notes**

*  The safety card is designed to create awareness about basic safety precautions.  
* The LED light provides a clear visual indication when the switch is pressed.  
* The card is made using simple and easily available materials.  
* It helps students understand the importance of safety through a simple working model.  
* The model is low-cost, simple to operate, and easy to demonstrate.
